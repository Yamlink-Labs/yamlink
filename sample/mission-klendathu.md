The Federation's first major offensive. The Sky Marshal committed everything — full fleet, full ground forces — based on the assumption that a single decisive strike would end the war.

It didn't. The Arachnids were ready. Fleet took massive losses from plasma bugs before anyone understood what was hitting them. Ground troops were dropped into a killing field.

[[dizzy-flores]] survived. [[johnny-rico]] survived. Most of the unit didn't. The Sky Marshal resigned the next day.

The Roughnecks were reformed afterward under [[lt-rasczak]], with the survivors from Klendathu as the core of the new unit.

## Notes

Military historians later called it one of the worst planned offensives in Federation history. At the time, the propaganda networks presented it as a strategic withdrawal.
