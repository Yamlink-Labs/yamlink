Federal Intelligence officer and telepath. Was in the same graduating class as [[johnny-rico]] and [[carmen-ibanez]] before the Bug War. While Rico went to Mobile Infantry and Carmen to Fleet, Carl joined Federal Intelligence.

Psychic — one of the most powerful telepaths in the Federation. Ran telepath operations on the Skinnies campaign. His intelligence work at [[mission-planet-p]] changed the direction of the entire war: he confirmed that Brain Bugs were coordinating Arachnid swarms, shifting Federation strategy to targeted decapitation strikes.

> [!SOURCE] Field debrief — Planet P
> Jenkins' post-mission report to SICON Command confirmed what field observers had suspected: coordinated Arachnid attack patterns indicated a command layer above warrior-class Bugs.

By the end of the war he outranked everyone he started with. Provided remote psychic support during [[mission-tango-urilla]] from orbit, reading residual Bug signals to guide the Roughnecks' approach.[^field-report]

[^field-report]: SICON Intelligence Summary, dated 2297-11-22. Classified for five years after Tango Urilla.

## Notes

The Federation never publicly acknowledged Carl's role in the intelligence break at Planet P. SICON preferred the narrative that it was conventional field reconnaissance.
