Rasczak taught Social Studies before the war. [[johnny-rico]] was one of his students. The teacher who went back in and made Lieutenant — one of the less unusual stories from the Bug War, but one of the more consequential ones.

Reformed the Roughnecks after Klendathu with the survivors as his core unit. Ran the unit as a meritocracy — everyone fought, nobody got special treatment, results were the only thing that mattered. It produced a very good fighting unit and some very loyal soldiers.

Killed at the outpost on [[mission-planet-p]] by Arachnid fire. Ordered his own unit to leave him rather than risk extraction under fire. [[johnny-rico]] obeyed the order.

The unit kept his name.

## Notes

"Figuring things out for yourself is the only freedom anyone really has." — Rasczak, before the war.
