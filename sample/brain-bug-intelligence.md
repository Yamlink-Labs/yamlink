# Brain Bug Intelligence Summary

Intelligence gathered from [[mission-planet-p]] and confirmed by [[carl-jenkins]] through psychic telepath contact. This dossier documents the evidence for a coordinated Arachnid command layer operating above warrior-class Bugs.[^classification]

[^classification]: This document was classified SICON-Eyes-Only until 2297-12-01. Declassified for historical record after Tango Urilla.

## Evidence

> [!SOURCE] Telepath contact — Carl Jenkins, [[mission-planet-p]] debrief
> Direct psychic contact with a Brain Bug specimen confirmed structured thought patterns consistent with operational command. The Brain Bug was processing tactical data from multiple concurrent engagements — not reacting, but coordinating.

> [!EVIDENCE] Field observation — Roughnecks after-action report
> Attack vectors on the Planet P outpost showed coordinated flanking behavior not previously documented in warrior-class encounters. Swarms avoided fields of fire in ways that suggested awareness of weapon placement from prior engagements.

> [!EVIDENCE] Signal analysis — SICON Intelligence
> Post-mission signal intercepts from the Planet P sector showed burst traffic consistent with hive coordination — patterns not observed in smaller Arachnid engagements.

> [!NOTE] Analytic note
> The Brain Bug does not fight. It thinks. Every warrior-class engagement near a Brain Bug installation shows coordinated behavior; every engagement far from one shows only instinct.

## Source Chain

Before Planet P, Federation command dismissed the idea of an Arachnid command structure. The evidence from this dossier, combined with [[carl-jenkins]]' telepath confirmation, shifted the entire Federation war strategy:

- targeting Brain Bugs becomes the primary strategic objective
- [[mission-tango-urilla]] was planned on coordinates from this intelligence
- SICON Command allocates dedicated telepath units to future ground operations
