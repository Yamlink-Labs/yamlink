Federation citizen. Volunteered for Mobile Infantry the same night as [[dizzy-flores]] and [[carmen-ibanez]], though for different reasons. None of them had any idea what they were getting into.

Rico didn't have the grades for Fleet. Ended up in Mobile Infantry as a Private, nearly quit after basic training, stayed after [[dizzy-flores]] was killed on Klendathu. Worked his way up through [[mission-klendathu]], [[mission-planet-p]], and the final assault on Tango Urilla.

By the end of the Bug War he was commanding the Roughnecks — the same unit he'd watched [[lt-rasczak]] lead from the front before Rasczak was killed on Planet P. Tied to [[carl-jenkins]]
