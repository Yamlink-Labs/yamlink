The Federation's space-based military branch. [[carmen-ibanez]] serves as pilot and a key Fleet officer during the Bug War, rising through the ranks aboard the Rodger Young.

The Fleet provides orbital support, troop insertion, and extraction for Mobile Infantry operations — including every major Roughnecks deployment.
