Follow-up operation after [[mission-planet-p]]. The Roughnecks deployed to Tango Urilla to act on intelligence recovered from the Brain Bug — specifically, coordinates suggesting a secondary hive network feeding into the Klendathu system.

[[johnny-rico]] led the assault. [[carl-jenkins]] provided remote psychic support from orbit, reading residual Bug signals to guide the unit's approach.[^intel-source]

The mission succeeded. The hive was destroyed, the network severed. No major Federation assets were lost.

> [!SOURCE] After-action intelligence summary — Carl Jenkins, SICON Command
> Psychic scan of the Tango Urilla hive confirmed severed coordination links to Klendathu. Brain Bug signal traffic dropped 94% within 48 hours of the strike.

> [!NOTE] Strategic significance
> Tango Urilla confirmed that targeting Brain Bug infrastructure, rather than fighting warrior-class Arachnids in open ground, was a viable and scalable strategy.

> [!WARNING] Casualty data reliability
> "Moderate" casualties is the official SICON designation. Field count from the Roughnecks' own after-action report is higher. The official number reflects survivors who returned to active duty within 30 days.

## Notes

Tango Urilla confirmed that the Federation's new strategy — targeting Brain Bugs and their coordination infrastructure rather than throwing bodies at warrior swarms — was viable. It was the first clean win after Klendathu.

> The Roughnecks went in expecting a fight. They came out with a victory and a lesson: precision beats mass when you're fighting something smart.
> — Post-mission debrief, Roughnecks, 2297-11-21
