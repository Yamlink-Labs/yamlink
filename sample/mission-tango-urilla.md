Follow-up operation after Planet P. The Roughnecks deployed to Tango Urilla to act on intelligence recovered from the Brain Bug — specifically, coordinates suggesting a secondary hive network feeding into the Klendathu system.

[[johnny-rico]] led the assault. [[carl-jenkins]] provided remote psychic support from orbit, reading residual Bug signals to guide the unit's approach.

The mission succeeded. The hive was destroyed, the network severed. No major Federation assets were lost.

## Notes

Tango Urilla confirmed that the Federation's new strategy — targeting Brain Bugs and their coordination infrastructure rather than throwing bodies at warrior swarms — was viable. It was the first clean win after Klendathu.
