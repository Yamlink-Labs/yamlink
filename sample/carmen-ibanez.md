Had the grades for Fleet. Became a pilot while [[johnny-rico]] went to Mobile Infantry. At first it seemed like she got the better deal.

Graduated top of her class. Assigned to the Rodger Young under [[zander-barcalow]], with whom she had a relationship that was simpler than the one she had with Rico and more honest about what it was.

Captured by Arachnids during the assault on Tango Urilla. Extracted by Rico's Roughnecks. The war had a way of pulling people back together.

## Notes

The Rodger Young took heavy casualties at Klendathu. Carmen was one of the officers who made it out.
