The Roughnecks were deployed to Planet P in response to a distress signal from a Federation research outpost. The signal turned out to be a trap — or at least, the Arachnids had been waiting.

[[dizzy-flores]] was killed early in the assault. [[lt-rasczak]] was mortally wounded at the outpost and ordered the unit to leave him. [[johnny-rico]] carried out that order and assumed command.

The Roughnecks held the outpost long enough for extraction. They captured enough intelligence from the facility to change the direction of the war — specifically, evidence that there were Brain Bugs coordinating the Arachnid swarms.

## Notes

Planet P was the mission that confirmed Bug intelligence. Before this, Federation command didn't believe the Arachnids had a command structure. After this, the strategy shifted to targeting Brain Bugs directly.

[[carl-jenkins]] later used Planet P intelligence to locate the Brain Bug on Klendathu.
