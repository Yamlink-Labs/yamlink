Played on the same football team as [[johnny-rico]] before the war. Chose Mobile Infantry specifically to follow him — a fact Rico didn't figure out until it was too late to matter.

One of the best soldiers in the Roughnecks. Kept her head in the chaos of [[mission-klendathu]] when half the unit was dead or pinned down. Transferred to [[lt-rasczak]]'s Roughnecks when the unit was reformed after Klendathu.

Killed by a Warrior Bug at the beginning of [[mission-planet-p]]. Rico held her at the end. The Roughnecks lost a lot of soldiers on Planet P — Dizzy was the one they didn't come back from.

## Notes

Rico figured out the thing about her too late. That happens a lot in war.
