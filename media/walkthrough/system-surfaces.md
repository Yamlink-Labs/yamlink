These are the wider workspace surfaces:

- **Home**: resume work and see the vault pulse
- **Calendar**: track due work and dated notes
- **Task Center**: every task in the vault, grouped by Overdue/Today/Upcoming/Undated/Done, with real mark-complete checkboxes
- **Vault Health**: inspect structure, drift, schema, and projections
- **Graph Workspace**: spatially inspect the connected system

You do not need to master them all right away. The goal is simply to see that your notes now behave like a system, not a pile of files.
