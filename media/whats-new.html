Fixed: the update notes themselves.

A quick one. The release notes you'd have seen after updating to 0.8.2 were stale — still describing 0.8.0, not the real calc-engine release that actually shipped. The real 0.8.2 content existed, it just never made it into the file this panel reads from. Fixed here; the real 0.8.2 story is one click away in the Releases list on the left. Nothing else changed in this version.

This panel was still showing the 0.8.0 briefing to anyone who updated to 0.8.2 — the real 0.8.2 content existed, drafted and approved, it just never got copied into the file this webview actually reads.
